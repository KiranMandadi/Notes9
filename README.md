How it works: if the Business Impact answer says customers, clients or a business function (trading, settlement, payments) could be affected, ChangeLens uses the Roll Back and User Restoration ETA to suggest an Impact:

restored within 15 min → 3 – Moderate
within 1 hour → 2 – Significant
over 1 hour, or no time given → 1 – Extensive

If the change’s Impact is lower than suggested, it is reported as a Major while awaiting approval. Statements of no impact, internal-only effects and non-production are ignored. Standard, Latent and Emergency changes are not checked.
